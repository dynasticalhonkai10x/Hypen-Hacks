How it works

Every challenge is measured.
Every place is real.

  1. 01

    See real challenges

    Live air-quality monitors (World Air Quality Index) and OpenWeather heat readings flag problems where they're measured. Readings older than a day are never used.

  2. 02

    Find green space within reach

    Real community gardens and allotments from OpenStreetMap are paired with the challenges nearest to them, with the actual distance shown.

  3. 03

    Commit and act

    Pick today's actions from the advisor, add the places you'll help to your own constellation, and earn badges for what you actually do.
